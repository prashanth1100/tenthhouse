About the studio

Behind every beautiful frame, there’s a storyteller.

Established in 2023 with a mission to revive classical studio portraiture — and to strengthen families and serve the community through soulful, lasting images.

Our story

What began as a passion for capturing authentic, emotion-rich portraits has grown into a creative collective hub — a space where art, stories and ideas find their home.

Every project here is built on collaboration, authenticity and craft. I work closely with clients, collaborators and community members to create work that resonates deeply and lasts.

  • 04

The founder

Abhijit Sunil Kalisetti

Photographer, storyteller and founder of Tenth House Studio. I believe each person has a unique story to tell, and I strive to capture it in a way that is timeless, authentic and meaningful.

I’m especially drawn to the classic portrait style, because it lets me create images that show not only the beauty of the individual but their personality and emotions too. I don’t claim to be the best photographer in town — but I am a constant learner, dedicated to improving my craft every day and giving my clients the best experience I can.
