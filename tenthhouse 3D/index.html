Director’s note

Behind every beautiful frame, there’s a storyteller.

I’m Abhijit Sunil Kalisetti — photographer, storyteller and founder of Tenth House Studio. I believe each person has a unique story to tell, and I strive to capture it in a way that is timeless, authentic and meaningful.

I founded the studio in 2023 to revive classical studio portraiture. I don’t claim to be the best photographer in town, but I am a constant learner — dedicated to improving my craft every day and giving every client the best experience I can.

Abhijit Sunil Kalisetti

Why choose us

An experience as considered as the photographs.

From the first message to the final print, every step is personal — in a studio built for comfort, light and storytelling.

  • 01Personalised experience

    Every shoot is customised around you — your story, your style and your pace.

  • 02Creative storytelling

    Each person has a unique story. Every session is planned to tell yours in a way that feels authentic and meaningful.

  • 03Professional studio

    A dedicated studio in Manikonda with professional equipment and a live preview system, so you see your images as we shoot.

  • 04Attention to every detail

    From posing and light to careful editing and retouching, every frame is considered.

  • 05Timeless visual quality

    A classic portrait style that shows personality and emotion — photographs that won’t date.

  • 06A comfortable experience

    A relaxed, welcoming space and flexible scheduling, so everyone — even the little ones — enjoys the shoot.
